LetsZikr Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

LetsZikr is a dhikr and tally counter application designed to work without requiring a user account.

Data Collection

LetsZikr does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

Local Data

Dhikr counters, custom counters, preferences, and application settings are stored locally on the user's device.

Advertising and Tracking

LetsZikr does not display advertisements and does not use advertising, analytics, or user-tracking services.

Permissions

LetsZikr does not require access to location, contacts, messages, photos, or other sensitive personal information.

Changes

If future versions of LetsZikr introduce features that collect or transmit data, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
